<h2>About This Portrait</h2>

I've been fascinated by indigenous peoples for as long as I can remember. They have something that we've lost. 
Instinct. 
Self-possession.
Resilience.
I saw a portrait of an African woman & was moved just by her countenance. She exhibited such [[strength]]. I had to draw her.
