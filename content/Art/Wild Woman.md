<h2>About This Portrait</h2>

I drew this for my therapist back in 2019. 
I was deeply appreciative of her help in my process of aligning with my [[truth]] & reconnecting with my instincts. 
The portrait represents to me the symbol of feminine power & [[embodiment]].
