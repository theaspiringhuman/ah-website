### I don't have answers, only [[art]].

On my morning walk yesterday I thought about how my life's message seems to have less to do with answers and more to do with making the questions themselves beautiful. 

For so long I've imagined that one day I would arrive at answers to all of my questions - that would be the reward for all of my earnest seeking - but I am seeing now that my reward is [[◉ Art is not an answer, it's a resonance|not an answer, but a gift of expression]]. 

**It is the gift of turning pain into [[beauty]].**
