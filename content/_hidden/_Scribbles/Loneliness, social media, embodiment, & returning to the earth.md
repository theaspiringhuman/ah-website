Being off of social media again has exposed how [[loneliness|lonely]] I am. That cheap sense of [[connection]] was masking the fact that I really have no one with who I am consistently my true self. I'm certain that I'm not the only one who feels this way. I know that most everyone in modern [[society]] is striving for true [[connection]]. Though we're surrounded by other people & have more access to each other than ever before, I believe we're also lonelier than ever. I think a lot of it has to do with what [[Charles Eisenstein]] talks about in Sacred Economics:

*(Crazy that I turned to this exact page when I opened the book just now.)*

> [!quote] "Human beings do not have an abstract need for long-distance communication. We have a need to stay in contact with people with whom we share emotional and economic ties. In past times, these people were usually close by. A hunter-gatherer or fourteenth-century Russian peasant would have had little use for a telephone. Telephones began to meet a need only when other developments in technology spread human beings further apart & splintered extended families & local communities." - pg. 97-98

---

& my dreams have been insisting that I get closer to the earth & begin inhabiting my life rather than simply observing & documenting it. I feel all of this is connected. My mind is not as skilled at cohesion as it once was, so I will have to sit with all of these fragments of [[meaning]] until they come together for me...
