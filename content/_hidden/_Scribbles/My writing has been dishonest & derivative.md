Up to now so much of my writing has been flat, derivative, & painfully cliche. I believe this is because for so much of my life I have been disconnected from myself and I have not trusted myself to say the things I truly feel and perceive. I have relied too heavily on the insights & assertions of others. There has been a lack of [[honesty]] in my writing & in my life. I have floated around ungrounded in the realm of idealism, neglecting the very real and interesting details just below me. Right in front of me. I have wished for my life to be something other than it is. I have wished for myself to be someone else. Someone braver, more confident, more educated, with a better upbringing & a sense of belonging in the world. 'Once I am brave & once I belong' I have vaguely imagined in my quiet hours, 'then I will be free to fully live.' But what better way to be brave than to tell the truth about myself? What better way to belong than to express my truest self, to let that authenticity radiate out into the world around me & to signal like a lighthouse to the other outsiders that here is another one of them?

I see this banality in a lot of the art I've done as well. At least the art I've created since I began to drift away from myself, around age 24. I used to create and even write from a place of deep [[authenticity]] and [[connection]] with myself and with [[God]]. I didn't think too much about what others would think of my creations because I wasn't creating for anyone but myself. I created for the sheer [[joy]] of creating. Once I began to try to make my art 'good' it all started to have this sort of forced feeling to it. I really lost the joy of creating and it shows.
