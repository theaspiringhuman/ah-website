A copy of a copy of a copy

is our world

Hand-me-down truths

That no one understands

Holograms and semblances

Of what we once were

And naked reality

Is too tender to touch

Divided and distracted

We cling

Each to his own piece

Of the puzzle

As if it were the whole picture
