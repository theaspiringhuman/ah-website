Date: 12-13-2023

Tags: [[healing]] #poems

Source: #Journals [[Desert Beige]]

---

I find myself seeking external validation. 

Reaching for [[love]].

Frozen in a place of longing.

The incense burns to dust & the clock ticks away, still the answers don't come & the ache in my heart remains.

Maybe the need isn't meant to be satiated but to be *felt*.

I need.

I long.

**I am not filled.**

I am empty, & I embrace this emptiness.

This hunger.

This restlessness.

I open myself to it all.

All that I haven't allowed myself to feel.

And though I cannot now see the fruits of this opening & allowing, I know that I am expanding my capacity to feel the depths of [[joy]] as well as this sorrow.

Because to open to the [[pain]] is to open to the bliss.

And as [[Carl Jung]] said,

*"The Tree that would grow to heaven must send its roots to hell."*
