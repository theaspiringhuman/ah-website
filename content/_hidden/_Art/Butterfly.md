<h2>About This Painting</h2>

Had a moment with a butterfly on a walk today

She sat still on the ground, wings closed

I extended my finger and gently touched her abdomen. She slowly lifted her head and gazed at me with kaleidoscope eyes.

She crawled with a sort of sluggishness onto my finger.

I thought maybe she couldn't fly. One of her wings had a piece torn from it.

We stared at each other for a long time.

Strong emotion welled up within me. I felt safe with this butterfly. I felt that she would sincerely listen if I spoke.

So I asked her in my heart, What are we doing here?

She stared thoughtfully back at me...

and then she asked me the same thing.

- June 16, 2023
