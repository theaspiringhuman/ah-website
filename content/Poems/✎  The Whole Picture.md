#### The Whole Picture

A copy of a copy of a copy

is our world

Hand-me-down [[truth|truths]]

That no one understands

Holograms & semblances

Of what we once were

And naked [[reality]]

Is too tender to touch

Divided & distracted

We cling

Each to their own piece

Of the puzzle

As if it were the whole picture
